<div class="about-section">
  <h2>🎯 My Story</h2>
  <p>My journey in computer science started in high school, where I fell in love with programming and electronics. That curiosity led me to a <strong>B.Sc. in Electronic and Computer Engineering</strong> (2015&ndash;2019) at the University of Trieste, where my first AI courses sparked a passion that shaped everything that followed &mdash; continuing with a <strong>M.Sc. in Computer Engineering</strong> (2019&ndash;2022) focused on Data Science and AI. A semester at <strong>Montanuniversit&auml;t Leoben</strong> through Erasmus+ pushed me out of my comfort zone: applying AI to energy and logistics taught me how transferable these methods really are.</p>
  <p>For my Master's thesis I turned to <strong>computer vision applied to the humanities</strong>, working alongside art historians and digital humanities experts. It was my first real contact with academic research, and I loved every part of it &mdash; engaging with the state of the art, designing evaluations, and digging into how modern models actually work. The thesis was later published in <strong>IEEE Access</strong> (2023) and convinced me to stay on this road.</p>
  <p>I continued with an <strong>Industrial Ph.D.</strong> (2023&ndash;2025), co-funded by PLUS S.r.l. within Area Science Park. I chose NLP &mdash; another field I had always loved &mdash; but my engineering mindset pulled me toward applied research useful for business. My thesis, <strong>Application of Large Language Models: Addressing Real-World Challenges</strong>, tackles three concrete questions: how to improve LLM outputs, how to evaluate them properly (with new metrics for Text-to-SQL), and how to make LLMs more sustainable through <strong>Green AI</strong>. Along the way I spent time as a visiting researcher at <strong>NOVA IMS in Lisbon</strong> and at <strong>UCL in London</strong>, attended 5 international summer and winter schools on NLP and ML, and published across journals, conferences and workshops.</p>
  <p>I am a proactive person with a deep desire to learn and push beyond my comfort zone. I believe that great engineering and great research share the same foundation: understanding a problem deeply, building solutions methodically, and measuring impact rigorously.</p>
