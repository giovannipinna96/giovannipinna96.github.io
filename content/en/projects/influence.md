## Influence — Marketing meets AI

**Influence** is a project that combines marketing strategies with artificial intelligence to analyze user data and generate social media content tailored to audience interests.

[![Influence project presentation](https://img.youtube.com/vi/NXmEs4L-rRg/0.jpg)](https://youtu.be/NXmEs4L-rRg?si=Q3pYlNx4kOm6DlGz)

### Features

- **Text Mining** — Extracts information from social media to identify audience-preferred topics and recurring keywords
- **Sentiment Analysis** — Evaluates public sentiment as positive, negative, or neutral
- **Social Network Analysis** — Predicts content appeal and estimates viral potential

### Background

Developed after completing the Digital Transformation course at the University of Trieste (July 2020) and further refined during the Contamination LAB competition, where it reached the finals.

### Technologies

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Machine Learning
- Social Media APIs
- Data Visualization
